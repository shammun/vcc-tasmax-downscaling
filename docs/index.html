Virginia CMIP6 Tmax at 4 km

Statistically downscaled CMIP6 daily maximum temperature for Virginia (about 4 km PRISM grid, 2015-2100; SSP2-4.5, SSP3-7.0, SSP5-8.5; 16-19 models). Bias correction by quantile delta mapping, residual analog downscaling (module v4.1.1).
