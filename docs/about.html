Virginia CMIP6 Tmax at 4 km

Statistically downscaled CMIP6 daily maximum temperature for Virginia (about 4 km PRISM grid, 2015-2100; SSP2-4.5, SSP3-7.0, SSP5-8.5; 16-19 models). Bias correction by quantile delta mapping, residual analog downscaling (module v4.1.1).

About

This site shows results of a statistically downscaled CMIP6 dataset of daily maximum near-surface air temperature (Tmax) for Virginia at about 4 km (the PRISM 1/24-degree grid), 2015-2100, under three scenarios: SSP2-4.5, SSP3-7.0 and SSP5-8.5, from 16 global climate models (19 for SSP5-8.5).

How the data were made

  1. Bias correction of each model's daily Tmax on its own grid against PRISM observations 1981-2014 (empirical quantile mapping per calendar month; for the future, quantile delta mapping in 30-year windows).
  2. Downscaling to 4 km with a residual analog method: a historical PRISM day with a similar coarse pattern provides the fine-scale detail, and the bias-corrected model value is kept exactly at the coarse grid points.
  3. Checks: every model and scenario passed quality control; the method was validated against PRISM 2015-2024.

How to read the results

  • Changes are relative to each model's own bias-corrected 1995-2014 climate; periods follow IPCC AR6 (2021-2040, 2041-2060, 2081-2100).
  • Ensemble values are medians of equally weighted models; ranges say which percentiles they are, and n (the number of models) is printed on every figure.
  • Scenario colours follow the IPCC AR6 style: SSP2-4.5 orange, SSP3-7.0 red, SSP5-8.5 dark red.

Limitations

The CMIP6 models are warmer than observed in the recent summer upper tail (about 90 % of that is inherited from the models, not from the downscaling), so counts of very hot days lean high; the ensemble is unconstrained and equally weighted; each model contributes one run. Details are on each result page.
